AI-Based Vehicle Damage Detection

The AI-Powered Vehicle Damage Detection System is an advanced machine learning application that automatically detects vehicle damages from uploaded images. Using image classification and object detection techniques, the system identifies damaged regions such as dents, scratches, cracks, broken headlights, damaged bumpers, and other external defects. The automated inspection process minimizes manual effort while improving assessment speed and accuracy.

The solution is designed for insurance companies, vehicle owners, and automobile service centers. AI-generated damage assessment reports help insurance assessors verify claims, estimate damage severity, reduce processing time, eliminate human errors, and enhance customer satisfaction throughout the insurance claim and vehicle maintenance process.
